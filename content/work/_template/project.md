## Approach

Each `## Heading` becomes its own section on the page and its own entry in the
list down the left-hand side. Add as many as you like, name them what you like.

Put a picture or a clip exactly where it belongs by naming it on its own line.
The text in the brackets becomes the caption, and anything referenced this way
is taken out of the gallery at the bottom:

![how the growth front advances](02-detail.jpg)

![the app running](03-demo.mp4)

You can use **bold**, *italic*, [links](https://example.com) and lists:

- one thing
- another thing
